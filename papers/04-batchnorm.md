id: batchnorm2015
title: Batch Normalization
authors: Ioffe, Szegedy
year: 2015
cites: sgd1998, covariate2009
---
We show that normalising layer inputs allows much higher learning rates and
makes initialisation far less delicate. The method achieves the same accuracy
with 14 times fewer training steps and beats the previous state-of-the-art on
ImageNet classification. We attribute the gain to reduced internal covariate
shift.
