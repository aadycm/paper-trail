---
id: smoothing2018
title: How Does Batch Normalization Help Optimization?
authors: Santurkar et al.
year: 2018
cites: batchnorm2015, covariate2009
---
We find that the success of batch normalisation has little to do with internal
covariate shift. However, it does make the optimisation landscape significantly
smoother, which is what allows the larger learning rates.
